About Felicity

16+ years of doing all of this & more.

With 16 years of experience and over 4,000 projects delivered, we believe our journey speaks for itself. But what truly makes us proud isn't just the number of projects we've completed — it's the lasting relationships we've built along the way.

Many of our earliest clients continue to be our most valued partners, while nearly half of our team has been part of our journey for close to a decade. That's a testament to the trust, consistency and passion that define who we are.

We don't believe in making big promises. We believe in showing up, giving our best and putting our heart into every project. Because, at the end of the day,

“WE GIVE EVERY PROJECT EVERYTHING WE'VE GOT.”
